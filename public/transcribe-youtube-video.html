Frequently asked questions

Is transcribing YouTube videos free?

Yes. You can transcribe for free every day: 1 video per day without an account, or 3 per day with a free Google sign-in. No credit card required.

How long can the video be?

Files up to 2 GB are supported — plenty for multi-hour videos, podcasts and lectures.

Do I also get subtitles (.srt)?

Yes. Besides plain text you can download your transcript as an .srt or .vtt subtitle file with real timecodes, ready for YouTube or your video editor.

Which languages are supported?

Transcriptr automatically detects 99 languages, including English, Dutch, German, French and Spanish. You can also translate foreign-language videos directly into English text.
