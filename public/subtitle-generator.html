Frequently asked questions

Do I need .srt or .vtt?

Use .srt for YouTube, video editors and most media players; .vtt is the standard for web (HTML5) players. Transcriptr gives you both.

Are the timecodes accurate?

Yes. The timecodes come straight from the speech recognition itself, per sentence — so your subtitles stay in sync with the video.

Can I make English subtitles from a Dutch video?

Yes. Turn on 'Translate to English' and download the English .srt — original timing preserved.

Is it free?

Yes. You can generate subtitles for free every day: 1 video per day without an account, 3 per day with a free Google sign-in.
