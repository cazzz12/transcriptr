Features
Everything you need, nothing you don't
Whisper AI engine
Powered by OpenAI's Whisper — the most accurate open-source speech recognition model available. Handles accents, background noise, and technical jargon.
Speaker diarization
Automatically identify and label different speakers in your recording. Perfect for interviews, meetings, and podcasts with multiple voices.
Timestamp precision
Get word-level timestamps or sentence-level markers. Sync your transcript to any media player or export as .srt subtitle files for video.
AI summary
Get an automatic 3–5 sentence summary of your content alongside the full transcript. Great for quick reviews before reading everything.
99 languages
Transcribe in Dutch, English, Spanish, German, French, Japanese, Arabic, Portuguese, and 91 more. Language is auto-detected — no manual selection needed.
Privacy first
Files are processed and deleted immediately after transcription. We never store your audio or transcripts on our servers without your explicit consent.
